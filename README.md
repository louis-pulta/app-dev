# Memento (2000)

> "Memory can change the shape of a room; it can change the color of a car. And memories can be distorted. They're only an interpretation, not a record."

## Overview
**Memento** is a critically acclaimed neo-noir psychological thriller directed by Christopher Nolan. The story follows **Leonard Shelby**, an insurance investigator who suffers from *anterograde amnesia* following a brutal attack that also claimed his wife's life.

### Key Narrative Features
* **Reverse Chronology:** The film's primary narrative unfolds in reverse order.
* **Dual Timeline:** 
  * *Color scenes* play backward, revealing how Leonard arrived at a specific moment.
  * *Black-and-white scenes* progress forward chronologically in a hotel room.
* **Investigation Tools:** Because he cannot form new long-term memories, Leonard relies on:
  * Polaroid photographs with descriptive captions
  * Custom skin tattoos detailing vital clues
  * Handwritten notes on polaroids and his clothes

### Technical Stack / Production Details
* **Director:** Christopher Nolan
* **Writer:** Christopher Nolan (based on the short story "Memento Mori" by Jonathan Nolan)
* **Starring:** Guy Pearce, Carrie-Anne Moss, and Joe Pantoliano
* **Runtime:** `113 minutes`
